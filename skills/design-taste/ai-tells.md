# AI tells (avoid unless the brief asks for them)

From taste-skill (MIT, Leonxlnx), condensed. These are the signatures of AI-generated pages. Any of them can be earned by the brief's own words; none of them is a default.

## Visual
- Neon or outer glows; oversaturated accents; gradient text on big headlines; custom mouse cursors
- AI purple/blue gradient over a dark mesh; glassmorphism on everything
- Three equal cards in a row; nested cards; soft-shadowed rounded rectangles standing in for content
- Colored `border-left` above 1px on cards or callouts; hard offset shadows outside a real neobrutalist world
- Decorative status dots before nav items, list rows or badges (only for real state, one per section)
- Crosshair or hairline grid lines drawn only for decoration; `border-top` + `border-bottom` on every row of a long list
- Progress bars with filled grey tracks as comparison visuals on a marketing page
- Div-built fake product UI (fake dashboards, task lists, terminals) and fake version footers inside them

## Labels and structure
- An **eyebrow** (small uppercase tracked label) above every section: at most 1 per 3 sections, hero included
- Section numbers (`01 / 02`, `001 · Capabilities`, `06 · how it works`) and image pagination (`01 / 4`)
- Version labels in the hero (`v0.6`, `BETA`, `EARLY ACCESS`) unless it's a launch
- "Left big headline + small floating paragraph top-right" section headers
- Decoration strips at the hero bottom (`BRAND. MOTION. SPATIAL.`); locale/time/weather strips (`LIS 14:23 · 18°C`)
- Scroll cues (`Scroll ↓`, animated mouse wheel)
- Pills or tags overlaid on photos; fake photo-credit captions (`Plate 03 · House archive`)
- Middle dots (`·`) as the separator for everything: max one per line
- Generic step labels (`Step 1 / Phase 01`): the step's verb is the label

## Copy
- **Em-dash (`—`) and en-dash as separator: zero** in visible text. Use a period, comma, colon, parentheses or a hyphen.
- Filler verbs: elevate, seamless, unleash, next-gen, revolutionize, empower
- Poetic-craftsman labels: "Field notes", "Quietly trusted by", "Currently on the bench", "From the field"
- Micro-meta sentences under headings ("Each of these is a feature we ship today…")
- Placeholder people and brands: John Doe, Sarah Chan, Acme, Nexus, SmartFlow; egg avatars
- Fake-precise numbers (`99.99%`, `4.1×`, `48k`) the brand never claimed
- Cute wordplay that doesn't parse; mixed registers (terminal mono + editorial prose + marketing punch) on one page
- Testimonials longer than 3 lines, or attributed by first name only

## Copy self-audit (before shipping)
Re-read every visible string: headlines, labels, buttons, captions, alt text, errors, footer. Rewrite anything grammatically broken, with an unclear referent, or that sounds like an LLM trying to sound thoughtful. Boring and clear beats cute and wrong.
